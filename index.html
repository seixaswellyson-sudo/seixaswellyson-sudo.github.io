About

I care about the unglamorous details: loading states, empty screens, the moment a form stops feeling like paperwork.

I started as a front-end developer, got impatient with ugly specs, and taught myself design so I could stop waiting for permission. Today I work across the whole arc — research, interaction design, visual systems, and the production code that keeps it honest.

My favourite problems live where engineering constraints meet human impatience: dense dashboards, onboarding tunnels, tools people use for eight hours straight.

Outside of work I restore mechanical keyboards, take long walks with a film camera, and lose at chess to people who are nicer about it than they should be.
